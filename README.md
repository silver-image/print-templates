# Print templates from Silver Image Print & Signs

Free artwork templates for print: **60 PDF templates for 10 products**, drawn at **1:1 in millimetres** with the bleed, the cut line and the safe zone marked. They are the same files offered on the service pages of [Silver Image Print & Signs](https://www.silverimagelondon.com), a print shop in London that is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

This repository also holds the **bleed, safe margin and resolution for 21 print products** as a table, CSV and JSON, and a **checklist to run before sending a print file**.

## How to use a template

1. Open the PDF for the finished size in your design application and build the artwork over the whole sheet.
2. Run backgrounds and pictures out to the bleed line; keep text and logos inside the safe zone.
3. Delete the template guide shapes before export.
4. Export a PDF with fonts embedded or outlined. A PDF with fonts embedded and 3 mm of bleed is the answer for nearly every job; the reasons are in the guide to [which file format to send to a printer](https://www.silverimagelondon.com/guides/what-file-format-for-printing).
5. For roller banners, the bottom 30 mm on the template is the strip that winds into the cassette: keep it clear of anything that has to be read. A file built at 1:10 is fine as long as it carries 300 dpi.

## Bleed, safe margin and resolution by product

Taken from the artwork requirements on each Silver Image Print & Signs service page, November 2026. The same table with the reason for each figure is in the guide to [how much bleed each print product needs](https://www.silverimagelondon.com/guides/how-much-bleed-does-each-print-product-need). Machine-readable copies: [CSV](specs/bleed-and-safe-margins.csv), [JSON](specs/bleed-and-safe-margins.json).

| Product | Bleed | Safe margin and other limits | Resolution at finished size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business cards , flyers , invitations , menus , place cards and ID badges | 3 mm on every edge | Text 5 mm clear of the trim | 300 dpi |
| Booklets , orders of service and documents | 3 mm on every edge | Text 5 mm clear of the trim | 300 dpi |
| Perfect bound books | 3 mm on all edges, inside and cover | Text 12 mm from the spine edge | 300 dpi |
| Wire bound documents | 3 mm on anything running to the edge | At least 15 mm clear on the bound edge | 300 dpi |
| Posters | 5 to 10 mm on every edge | Text and logos 5 mm inside the trim | 150 dpi |
| A-board posters | 3 mm on every edge | Text and numbers 15 mm inside the edge | 150 dpi |
| Foamex boards , strut cards and table plans | 5 to 10 mm on every edge | Text and logos 5 mm inside the trim | 300 dpi |
| Correx | 5 to 10 mm on every edge | Text 10 mm inside the trim, clear of eyelets and holes | 150 dpi |
| Dibond | 5 mm on every edge | Text 15 mm inside the trim, 25 mm clear of screw corners | 150 dpi; 300 for plaque-scale text |
| Safety signs | 5 to 10 mm on every edge | Text and symbols 5 mm inside the trim | 150 dpi |
| PVC banners | 5 to 10 mm on every edge | Important detail 50 mm from the edges and eyelets | 30 dpi is enough |
| Roller banners | 5 to 10 mm on every edge | Text and logos 5 mm inside the trim | 30 dpi is enough |
| Exhibition graphics | 3 mm per panel edge | 20 mm top and bottom; faces and text off seam lines | 150 dpi |
| Hoarding panels | 5 mm per panel edge; 50 mm or more top and bottom on a slope | Critical content 150 mm off the top and bottom | 30 dpi at the finished size, which is 300 dpi in artwork built at 1:10 |
| Canvas | Bar depth plus 20 mm on every edge | Faces, text and logos well inside the wrap line | 150 to 300 dpi |
| Printed tablecloths | 10 mm on every edge | Logos out of the corner folds | 150 dpi |
| Feather flags | 10 mm | Text and logos 50 mm clear of the mast-side edge | 150 dpi |
| Stickers | 3 to 5 mm beyond the cut path | Text and detail 3 mm inside the cut path | 150 dpi |
| Vinyl graphics | 3 to 5 mm beyond the cut path on printed graphics | Strokes and gaps no finer than about 3 mm on cut lettering | 150 dpi; vector for lettering |
| Window film | 10 to 15 mm on full-pane coverage | Each pane measured at the top, middle and bottom | 150 dpi |
| Vehicle decals and magnetic panels | 3 mm | Around 15 mm cap height for cut letters | Vector for anything cut |

Bleed is the part of the artwork that runs past the finished edge and is cut off; the safe margin is the strip inside the edge where text is kept out. A file built at a reduced scale needs proportionally more resolution: artwork drawn at 1:10 and 300 dpi lands at 30 dpi once it is printed at full size. Vector artwork carries no resolution and is the better send for logos, lettering and anything that will be cut.
